FACE Size

The Adapt 3 comes in two fits. The standard frame is sized for medium to large faces, and the Adapt S is built for smaller faces. Same lens, same features, just a better seal for your face.

Nose Bridge

The Adapt 3 comes in a standard fit and a low-bridge fit. Low-bridge is shaped for a lower nose bridge and higher cheekbones to reduce gaps and improve comfort. If goggles usually leave a gap at your nose, go with low-bridge.

Lens shape

The Fathom 2 comes in two lens shapes. Toric lenses curve in two directions to match the shape of your eye, which reduces distortion and gives you a more natural view at the edges. Cylindrical lenses give you similar optical quality with a clean, classic look.
